FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

YOUR QUESTIONS, ANSWERED.

Do I need previous modelling experience to attend the Mega Workshop?+

No. The workshop is intended to introduce aspiring talent to modelling fundamentals, confidence and industry awareness.

What is the difference between the one-day workshop and the full BK EDU course?+

The Mumbai Mega Workshop is a one-day introductory experience on 22nd November 2026. The full course has the broader modules outlined above, including runway, posing, grooming, social media and industry preparation.

What topics are covered in BK EDU’s course?+

The presentation outlines personality development, aesthetics and appearance, ramp walk, posing, styling, social media, industry readiness and media attention.

Will I receive international exposure or a placement after joining?+

BK EDU presents industry exposure as part of its broader offering. Specific international opportunities, placements and eligibility should be confirmed with the admissions team; participation alone does not guarantee selection.

Where is BK EDU available, and how can I enrol?+

BK EDU lists its presence in Mumbai, Bengaluru, Kolkata and Dubai. Use the workshop registration CTA to reserve a Mumbai workshop seat, or contact the team for full-course admissions and fees.
